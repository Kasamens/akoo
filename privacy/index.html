Privacy

What we collect

  • Your learning progress, which is saved in your browser.
  • Feedback you choose to send, with the page you were on, basic details about your device, and anything you add, such as your name.
  • Anonymous visit counts that show which pages and lessons are used.
  • Offline copies of Akoo's own pages, and of any podcast episode you play or save, kept in your browser so lessons work without a connection. They hold nothing about you, and clearing this site's data removes them.

Why

To run Akoo and improve it.
