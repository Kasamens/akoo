Plain-language privacy

Privacy

Akoo has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Here is everything it keeps, and where.

Your progress

Finished lessons, quiz scores, your last lesson and your Daylight/Dusk choice are saved only in your browser (local storage). They are never sent to us. You can back them up, restore them or delete them from Your progress in the lessons menu.

Feedback you send

When you use Squawk at us, we receive:

  • the kind of feedback and your message;
  • the course and lesson you were on;
  • your device type (phone, tablet or laptop), browser and system name, light or dark theme, and the time;
  • your name, only if you tick “Add my name to the Thanks page”;
  • how many lessons you've finished, only if you tick that box.

We never ask for your email address, and we don't collect your IP address or any other identifier. Please don't put personal details in your message.

Where it goes, and why

Feedback is emailed to Akoo's own mailbox (a Google Gmail account) through a small Google Apps Script. We copy it into a private feedback log, which we use with the help of Claude, an AI assistant, to sort and track what to fix. We use feedback only to improve Akoo. We don't sell it, share it or use it for marketing.

The Thanks page

If your feedback leads to a change, it may appear on the Thanks page. Your name appears there only if you chose to add it. Otherwise the card shows only your feedback number.

How long we keep it

  • Feedback messages: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Names on the Thanks page: for as long as the card is shown, or until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

To have your name removed from the Thanks page, or your feedback deleted, email akoobird@gmail.com and include your feedback number (for example AK-0042). We'll do it promptly.

We follow the principles of the EU GDPR and Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843): consent, collecting as little as possible, and deleting on request. Last updated 25 November 2026.
